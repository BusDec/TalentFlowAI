The Recruitment Challenge

Public sector recruitment faces unique challenges that traditional systems can't solve

📋

Manual Document Verification

Thousands of applications require manual verification of qualifications, experience, and identity documents — taking weeks of staff time.

🔍

Eligibility Screening

Complex eligibility criteria across age, education, experience, and category require careful manual review for each application.

📊

Reservation Compliance

DoPT roster requirements, category-wise vacancies, and PwBD horizontal reservations add layers of complexity to shortlisting.

⏱️

Long Processing Times

From advertisement to joining, government recruitment cycles take 6-12 months, losing top candidates to private sector.

🔒

Data Security & Privacy

Aadhaar, PAN, and personal documents require DPDP Act compliance with encryption at rest and access controls.

📝

Audit & Transparency

RTI requirements demand complete audit trails for every decision — who changed what, when, and why.

How TalentFlow Solves It

AI-powered automation with human oversight at every step

🤖 AI Document Parsing

OCR + LLM extraction of qualifications, experience, and identity from uploaded documents with per-field confidence scores.

✅ Automated Eligibility

Rule-based screening against structured criteria — age, education, experience, certificates — with human override capability.

📊 Roster Management

DoPT 100-point roster tracking with category-wise vacancy allocation and automatic breach warnings.

⚡ Async Processing

Celery-powered background tasks for resume parsing, evaluation, and duplicate detection — instant application acknowledgment.

🔐 PII Encryption

Fernet encryption at rest for Aadhaar and sensitive data, masked display in admin, DPDP Act compliant.

📝 Audit Trail

Immutable AuditEvent model tracking every status change, score update, and override — exportable for RTI responses.

🎯 Smart Shortlisting

AI-powered candidate ranking with tie-breaking rules, category-aware allocation, and fairness metrics.

📄 Govt-Format PDFs

Publication-ready advertisement PDFs, application slips, and offer letters matching government notice formats.
